# Imagens e identidade visual

Referência: imagem de layout fornecida pelo usuário em 7 de outubro de 2026. A interface foi construída em HTML/CSS/React, sem usar uma captura de tela como site.

Ferramenta: imagegen nativa, sem CLI/API de geração. Saídas selecionadas foram convertidas para WebP; o contato de seis fotos foi dividido em seis arquivos, sem alterar o conteúdo visual de cada cena.

## Hero

Arquivo final: `public/images/hero.webp`.

Prompt utilizado:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: wide website hero background for Brazilian hip hop web radio Deluxe. Create a cinematic editorial photograph, landscape 1536x1024 or wider: a Black male hip hop DJ in black baseball cap, dark sunglasses and black hoodie wearing large premium black headphones, on the right half of composition, face in profile facing right toward a vintage golden broadcast microphone in foreground right. Dark gritty recording studio with city skyline and subtle graffiti textures, yellow/gold rim lights, deep black shadow and monochrome gray with warm gold highlights. Left half should be mostly dark atmospheric negative space for white HTML headlines. Authentic hip hop culture, film grain, realistic fabrics and equipment. No words, no logos, no lettering, no UI, no watermark. Save the generated image locally for use in a website.

## Cenas editoriais

Arquivos finais: `public/images/rapper.webp`, `crowd.webp`, `city.webp`, `vinyl.webp`, `battle.webp` e `speakers.webp`.

Prompt utilizado:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: photographic contact sheet for six separate hip hop radio editorial images. Create an exactly regular grid of 3 columns by 2 rows, six equal rectangular photographs edge-to-edge, with NO spacing, NO borders and NO text. Landscape overall 1536x1024. Top left: Black male Brazilian rapper holding a studio microphone, black cap, black and white moody portrait. Top middle: energetic hip hop concert crowd and hands in the air, stage backlight, monochrome photo with warm golden highlights. Top right: hooded silhouetted young person overlooking Brazilian urban skyline at sunset, orange and dark charcoal. Bottom left: black vinyl record spinning on a turntable, warm yellow studio light, tight detailed photo. Bottom middle: two Brazilian rappers in a freestyle battle at a gritty graffiti-covered street wall, warm muted tones. Bottom right: tower of large vintage speakers and a boombox in a dark underground studio. Each image should completely fill its sixth of the image, same dark cinematic gritty hip hop editorial art direction. No brands no words no text no logos no watermark.

## Marca

Marca tipográfica provisória em fonte Permanent Marker, com coroa vetorial e identificação Web Rádio. Favicon SVG próprio em preto e amarelo. Substituir pela identidade oficial quando fornecida.
